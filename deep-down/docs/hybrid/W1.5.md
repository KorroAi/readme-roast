## 1. The visual rules (1C and 1E share them; every other owner respects them)

1. **Clear air.** No global haze. Volumetric in-scatter appears only inside real beams: the sky shaft, a lamp's cone, the breach
   leak, and at most a faint halo within 1.5 m of a practical. The air within 10 m stays clear in a chamber at rest.
   - Fog: height and distance fog only beyond 12–18 m, in the biome's dark hue.
   - Caps: fog never fills the sky or the near field; the far fog colour never exceeds 0.25 display.
2. **A readable lantern pool.**
   - Size: a warm pool about 6–8 m across, lit stone at 0.55–0.75 display at 1.5–2 m.
   - Fill: the ambient fill is cool and hued (fillGain 0.55–0.6); darks stay at 0.04–0.10 display, never flat black over a
     large area.
3. **One lit focal point per frame:** a relit lamp, a crystal cluster, the sky shaft, the next exit, or the treasure.
   - The suggestion is the brightest object.
   - Cold lamps read as unlit: no flame, dark glass. Only a relight gives a flame.
4. **Two hues and a warm counterpoint per biome.**
   - Topsoil: warm amber lamps against blue-dusk stone, with sky-blue light from the shaft.
   - Glowroot: dark blue-black and green-black stone with green and teal accents **only** on roots, bulbs and crystals, plus
     amber Halden lamps.
   - Never a frame that is all one hue.
5. **Crisp, chunky, high-definition stone in every chamber**, like the spike:
   - bevels, strata bands, cobbles or blocks, cracks, rubble at wall feet;
   - the HD painter visible on walls, floors and ceilings, with no blur and no stair-steps;
   - a stone texel ≤ 1 scene pixel at 1 m.
6. **Detail:** each chamber gets ≥ 12 distinct dressing props in view from its arrival pose: timber, rails, crates, barrels,
   rope, chains, roots, dripstone, rubble, pebbles, plaques, runes, puddles. Nothing floats; everything sits on a surface.
7. **Calm:** SPEC §8.7–§8.8 are unchanged.
8. **Budget:** ≤ 120 draw calls and ≤ 150k triangles on Balanced at every stand, with props merged or instanced and
   `noShadow` on small decor. A shadow refresh stays ≤ 60 extra calls. High must fit ≤ 16 ms modelled: cut MSAA and scale on
   High rather than features.
