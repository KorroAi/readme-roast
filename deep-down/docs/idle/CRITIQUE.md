# Critic panel on SPEC.md and BUILD.md (apply during the build)

Three critics reviewed the spec against the director's demands. The feasibility critic was stopped when the director asked to start building at once. Each specialist applies the MUST items that touch their workstream and lists in their report what they applied. A MUST item that changes another workstream's files goes in `requests`.

## Critic
**Scores:** Truly addictive; you never get bored 6/10; Ultra simple (one thumb, portrait, no joystick) 7/10; Punchy and fast (nerveux), thrilling (palpitant) 7/10; Endless and evolving, with endless upgrades up to absurdity (démesure) 5/10; Many more things to do, with entertaining and varied loops 6/10; A guided tutorial and an objective always shown; nothing unexplained 8/10; Caverns never empty, with wonder and discovery 7/10; A big visual upgrade: much more beautiful, HD, polished 7/10; A REAL feeling of progression, improved in depth 6/10; An addictive upgrade mechanic 7/10; Achievements 8/10; A better UI with real, high-quality animations and effects 8/10; It triggers dopamine while you play 8/10

**Why (lowest scores):**
- Endless and evolving, with endless upgrades up to absurdity (démesure) (5): The numbers never end (Big, letters up to 1e2091), crew and Echo lines keep coming, and the Loom nodes and Breach tiers are unlimited. The visible absurdity stops early. The 15 named tools end at Pick L350, about 1,500 m, which is run 2 on day 1. After that you get 'Lantern Edge N' with an aura that grows 0.3 m per tier and has no cap. Echo crew lines are recolours at ×1.15 scale. On day 7 the depth plate reads 8.3 km, shallower than the Kola borehole. Echo strata are re-tints. The counter's tier colours run out at Qi (1e18) in the first hour.
- Truly addictive; you never get bored (6): The first 45 minutes are excellent: 8 biomes, 2 Rekindles, a new mechanic at least every 4 minutes and gaps of 30 s or less (E5, E17). After that it falls apart. The SPEC's own sim (§5.12) gives +100–350 m a day on days 4–7, with an Echo stratum every 1,100 m, so only one new stratum arrives between day 3 and day 7. The mystery ends at the Gate around day 2. Every Rekindle replays the same 14 gates, tool names and crowns. Breach, the stated fix for the long tail, is still only a target (E10) and has not been simulated. Holding a thumb (or three fingers) on the glass beats tapping. As a player I quit around day 4.
- Many more things to do, with entertaining and varied loops (6): The list in §0 is long, but most items are meters that fill on their own (Feats, Rank, Codex, contracts that track what you already do). The verbs you actually perform: tap, buy, press a skill when it is ready, mash a Warden, five one-tap widgets (mostly Catch), and pick 1 of 3. The side loops that had decisions of their own (Forge combos, Rift Dives, camp building) were cut in §1. Nothing replaces them until Breach on day 2.
- A REAL feeling of progression, improved in depth (6): There are many layers and the goal bar is always about 55 % full. But the PICK card sits in quick-buy slot 1 forever while the tap is dominated by Rally × crew DPS. By my estimate the pick term is about 10 % of tap damage at 240 m and about 0.2 % at 400 m, so the headline upgrade is dead 8–15 minutes into every run. The ghost lantern and green splits are automatic wins, because L7 guarantees every run is faster. Rank XP 'from every cell' explodes during re-climbs. The Dig sheet's caps (Rally 20, Lucky 25, Steady 6, Bellows 10) end tap growth early in each run.

**MUST change:**
1. §3.1 input: holding, swiping and multiple fingers must not beat rhythmic tapping. Today three held fingers give 3 × 5 = 15 taps/s with no effort, triple the 5 taps/s the sim is calibrated on. Count at most 8 taps/s across all pointers combined (not 15). Make the hold auto-tap one global stream, not one per finger: 3/s base, Steady Hand up to 5/s, Surge 8/s. Taps from a hold give half combo and half Surge meter. Swipe hits count against the same 8/s cap. Update F5, and add an E20 variant in which a hold-only bot and a three-finger bot do not reach a greater depth at 30 min than the 5 taps/s tapping bot.
2. §3.2/§5.4/§9.1 the PICK must never be a dead slot. With tapDmg = pick + rally × crewDPS, the pick term falls to about 10 % of the tap at 240 m and about 0.2 % at 400 m (my estimate from the costs and HP in §5.2 and §5.4). Either fold Rally into the Pick, so each Pick tier raises the crew-DPS share of every tap, replacing the 20-level Rally card and recalibrating so E20 stays at 2–4. Or remove the permanent PICK slot: quick-buy becomes [GEAR/NEXT UNLOCK] [BEST] [NEWEST CREW], and pick tiers become one-shot tool forms priced in rows of gold at the current depth. Acceptance: in a 2 h and a 7-day sim, slot 1's delta preview is +1 % or more in at least 95 % of 0.5 s samples.
3. §2.3/§5.12 content cadence after day 2: one new stratum between day 3 and day 7 is a drought. Make Echo strata record-relative or 550 m apart, and re-run the economy sim. Add E26: in a 14-day session sim, every play day brings at least one new biome, stratum or prestige unlock, and every 6-minute session at least one lifetime-first event. Move 'tools/econ-sim days 14 with Breach passes E10 and E26' to the W2 gate (BUILD §3.4), so that Breach is calibrated before W3 builds it.
4. §5.9 Breach must never cost progress (L11). Today 'each challenge is a fresh Weave cycle' wipes the current Embers, and the bot enters a challenge 'when no Weave is recommended'. Specify instead: entering a Breach saves the current cycle (run, Embers, Threads); the challenge runs under its rule; reaching the goal or tapping Return at any time restores the saved cycle; offline time accrues to the saved cycle. Add this to noPunishment.test.
5. §6.2 Plunge and analytic rows, plus §4 offline: define the accounting, or a re-climb from 10 % of a 4,500 m record breaks about 28,000 cells. Rank XP and the Breaker Feat would then jump around 8 ranks per re-climb (need(r) = 120·r^0.78), and chest cells would fire 1+ roll-ups a second. Rule: XP, Finds and Feat cell counters count only taps and cells below the record. Chest cells passed in a Plunge go to the Chest Rack (10 max, the rest to gold), with pity advancing once per rack slot. Planted lanterns are collected automatically when passed. Facet hands are merged (see shouldChange). Test: rank stays within E19-style bands at day 3 and day 7 in the session sim, and E25 holds with these sources included.
6. §7.7 ghost lantern and splits: by L7 every run is faster, so the ghost is always beaten and every split is green, which makes 5 gems × 14 a run automatic. Race against a par instead: the sim's projection for your current power (the same 30 ms run the Rekindle screen already does). The ghost runs at par, and a green split (and its gems) requires beating par by at least 5 %.
7. §8.8/§9.1 HUD overlays must not steal dig taps (L2). First-time cards and toasts (rect 24,173,342,52) sit inside the world region and open the Codex on tap. Make them pointer-events: none while play is running, and reach the Guide through a 48 px '?' chip in the skill row or through the Codex. Declare the Depth Ruler and event bubbles non-interactive. Extend F2: 500 random world taps made while a first-time card, a Feat toast and an event bubble are on screen all dig.
8. §3.1/§6.6/§7.2 thumb zone: limit the reach band to 4 m above and 4 m below the floor. Today it runs from 6 m above to 10 m below, but the view shows only 5.2 m above and 4.8 m below. Keep the paths of the Lucky Moth, Catch targets, the Treasure Mole and creatures, and the Warden weak points, inside the lower 65 % of the world region (y ≥ 330 at 390 × 844). Add this to U4 at all three viewports.
9. §8.2/§8.3 the tutorial timeline must match the economy. A first ★ at 0:20 is impossible: Shovel Hand L10 costs 797 gold (50·(1.1^10−1)/0.1), Pick L10 costs 624, and you have only earned about 637 gold by 20 m (50·(1.14^20−1)). The sim's 'first crew 5 s' contradicts 'first purchase 5 s' at 30 + 50 gold. Re-time every scripted step from tools/econ-sim first at 3 and 5 taps/s, and make G1 assert each step's time within ±25 %.
10. §3.3 tool forms: split the visible named form from the ×2.5 damage tier. Damage tiers stay every 25 levels. The 15 named models unlock on lifetime milestones spread across week 1 (for example one per new biome or stratum record), because Pick L350 is reached on day 1. Cap the Lantern Edge aura logarithmically, at most 1.2 × the shaft width (today +0.3 m per tier reaches about 18 m by day 7).
11. §2.4/§6.8 make depth itself feel absurd. Add one-time depth cards (Burj 828 m, Mponeng 3.9 km, Everest 8,849 m, Kola 12,262 m, Moho, the mantle, Earth's core) and a display-only metres-per-row scale that grows ×10 per Weave or per Echo cycle (HP maths untouched, as endless.md's rule 4 says). The depth plate should read more than 1,000 km by day 7 and keep accelerating. Show the scale change as a T4 'the deep stretches' ceremony.

**SHOULD change:**
1. §3.4 Surge: when the meter is full, it waits up to 8 s for a tap on the ring before firing by itself. That lets players save it for a Warden or an event, which is a real decision and a thrill.
2. Add a beat layer that rewards rhythm: the lantern pulses on the music's beat, and a tap within ±80 ms of a beat counts double for combo and Surge (not for damage). Tapping becomes musical and skilful, and holding falls behind naturally.
3. §7.1 Seal HP: base it on depth (for example 25 × row HP at the boundary), clamped between 8 s and 25 s of the arrival DPS. A player who arrives over-levelled breaks it in a few taps with an 'OVERPOWERED' stamp, and upgrades show in the fight.
4. §7.3 events: add two widget verbs (a Hold-and-release charge with a sweet spot, and a 1-of-3 'door' with honest visible odds where the floor is kept), and cap Catch at 30 % or less of the cards the Director deals in any 10.
5. §3.2 leftover crumble: crumbled cells pay full loot only for rock. Ore, rich, rare and chest cells must be tapped, or they crumble at 50 %. Today the optimal play is to avoid the shiny cells and let them fall for free.
6. §5.10 facets: during re-climbs below the record, bank the charted-gate hands and offer one draft of N picks when you cross the record line, with at most one hand per 2 minutes otherwise. 13 hands a run times 7 runs a day is choice fatigue.
7. §5.4 crew: each crown on an older line gives the newest line +5 %, so the 14 lines below the top one stay worth buying and the Crew sheet isn't 90 % dead rows.
8. §5.7 gems: put Lantern Oil and Time Warp into the sim at the expected gem income (by my estimate 200–500 gems a day buys roughly 4–10 h of ×2 a day), cap Oil at one active stack of 2 h, and add a gem sink that scales (for example surface camp buildings with 10 levels each).
9. §8.2/§8.3 move the Board, Codex and Feats tabs after the first Warden (4 min or later). The first 3 minutes should teach at most 12 things that act on the dig; E17 still has room.
10. §10.8 the pick-tier T4 and crown T3 ceremonies play in full only the first time per tier ever. Later runs show a T2 chip ('Iron Pick ✓'), like biomes already do (T5 first, T3 later).
11. §5.1 counter tier colours: keep climbing past Qi. Give the counter a new frame every 1e30 of lifetime gold through the letter pairs, so the counter still evolves in week 2.
12. §8.4 show the current Path step as a persistent second line under the depth plate, because the goal bar mostly shows ★ micro-goals and the real objective gets lost.
13. §6.5 every mutator should change play, not only the palette: each one adds one mutator-specific event and one set-piece overlay with a tappable, so an Echo stratum is more than a recolour.
14. §5.4 skills: auto-skills (1,000 Embers) should hold the other three until Earthquake is ready and fire Cataclysm. Otherwise automation silently deletes Cataclysm.
15. §6.2 inconsistency: the Plunge 'exits when a biome boundary is crossed', yet it also 'flashes each biome boundary crossed'. Pick one. For re-climbs, keep plunging through charted gates and flash the name.
16. §4.1 idle frame cap: keep 30 fps as the floor while the app is in the foreground and Battery saver is off, because players watch their crew and 20 fps looks cheap.
17. BUILD §3.2/§4.4: split W2-B into two waves, or cut it to 7 biomes plus Echo. Add an art gate with a reference paintover per biome, judged by three critics on a 0–10 rubric (readability, wow, cohesion, ≥ 7 to pass) on top of V1–V10.
18. BUILD §4.4: next to the 15-minute cold play-through, play accelerated day-3 and day-7 saves for 15 minutes each and log every boring or repeated moment, because the problems listed here only appear after hour 1.
19. §5.1 nit: 'the first cell pays 1' contradicts rock at 0.6×. Author row 0 as a special cell, or fix the text.

## Critic
**Scores:** Truly addictive; you never get bored 7/10; Ultra simple to use (one thumb, portrait, no joystick) 7/10; Punchy and fast (nerveux), thrilling (palpitant) 7/10; Endless and evolving, with endless upgrades up to absurdity (démesure) 7/10; Many more things to do, with entertaining and varied loops 8/10; A guided tutorial and an objective always shown; nothing unexplained 8/10; Caverns never empty, with wonder and discovery 6/10; A big visual upgrade: much more beautiful, HD, polished 5/10; A REAL feeling of progression, improved in depth 7/10; An addictive upgrade mechanic 8/10; Achievements 7/10; A better UI with real, high-quality animations and effects 7/10; It triggers dopamine while you play 7/10

**Why (lowest scores):**
- A big visual upgrade: much more beautiful, HD, polished (5): The ingredients are right: native composite with CAS, LUT looks, HD section layers, GPU particles, BSP shards, backdrops and light cones. But there are feasibility holes. The light volume ends at z = 0, so everything in front of the cut samples clamped solid-rock light. The lantern cannot light the section. The rim is drawn from a 1 m distance field while the outline is sculpted by up to 0.5 m. A 16³ linear LUT loses the brand's dark grading. There is no edge AA under a permanently swaying camera. The native composite and CAS arrive only in W3, and nothing is measured on a GPU. Art capacity is far too small, and there is no beauty rubric beyond luminance statistics.
- Caverns never empty, with wonder and discovery (6): The fill contract, density targets and fill-scan tests are strong. But the biome reveal dives the camera 'into the Vista', while the generator puts the Vista 35 % into the biome, so the money shot has no cavern under the seal. The density tests count props without judging how they look. About 30 set pieces, Wardens and fill heroes plus 42 creatures are each described in one line and given almost no build time.
- Truly addictive; you never get bored (7): Six reward clocks, a Director with deck rules and pity, novelty at least every 4 min, a boredom breaker and a find guarantee: the structure is there. Visually it is at risk. About 45 % of the screen is a flat textured section plane. Its legibility and variety depend on look-dev time that BUILD does not budget. Echo strata only rotate emissive hue and the LUT over 13 biomes, so the visuals go stale long before the numbers do.
- Ultra simple to use (one thumb, portrait, no joystick) (7): Taps only, a column mapping that ignores vertical position, the thumb rule and 48 px targets are all strong. The HUD is dense: 6 tabs, 4 skills, combo/Surge, a facet chip, 3 quick-buy cards, the goal bar, the ruler, bubbles and 7 top-bar readouts. The event bubbles (x 8–56) also overlap column 0 inside the world region: 15 px at 390 and 30 px at 360. That breaks the rule that nothing interactive sits over the shaft.

**MUST change:**
1. Light apron (SPEC §6.2/§11.3; RingWorld.ts, world/light.ts, lighting.ts). Extend the light and lantern volumes 4 voxels in front of the cut plane, either as storage z −4…63 or by moving the cut to storage z = 4 with z 0–3 permanently AIR. Today a section face samples uLightTex at z = −0.5, and ClampToEdge maps that onto the solid z = 0 slice (light 0, openness 0). Every shard, loot pop, dust particle, chest popped to z −2 m and Warden in front of the cut is lit by that clamped value. Then add one light-volume fetch at vGrid + n·0.5 to the section path (3 → 4 fetches), so fissure emitters, gem glows, lamps and 'the glow below' actually colour the section. Tests: a unit test that a voxel at z = −1 beside an emitter at z = 0 receives light, and a capture check that the section within 2 m of a gem cell is ≥ 1.2× brighter than 4 m away.
2. Move the dig lantern in front of the cut (§6.9, §11.1 pillar 5). The foreman's pole lantern sits at z ≈ 1, behind every section face (normal 0,0,−1). So dot(n,l) < 0, and lanternLight() leaves only its ~5 % wrap: the warm pool on the dig face cannot exist. Hang the lamp in the apron at x = the foreman's column, y = floor + 1.2 m, z = −0.8 m (on a boom or a cable). Centre LanternField there. Specify the section lantern term: wrap 0.35 on −z faces, radius 9 m. Gate V7 by capturing with the lantern on and off: on must add ≥ 0.25× the dig band's luminance.

Instead, scale the hit cell's front face and its ledge face about the cell centre in x/y to 1.08 (about 3.5 px each side) on the kick spring, peaking at F2, with a 1 px darker outline and the flash; crits use 1.14. Add an F-test: the projected face width at F2 is ≥ 1.06× rest.
4. Align the rim with the drawn outline (§11.2, SectionField). SectionField has 1 texel per metre, so its bilinear 0.5 iso-line follows the voxel grid. But the mesher moves cut-plane corners by up to 0.5 m (OFFSET_RANGE 1.0 × 0.5), so the rim and the depth-into-rock key float off the real edge. Either set the cut-plane xy offsets to 0 in the shaft footprint ±3 cells and ≤ 0.15 m elsewhere, or bake a per-vertex 'distance to the air outline' in the mesher for z = 0 faces and drive the rim from it. Extend V9: rim pixels lie within 3 px of the geometric outline.
5. Make the dig band a designed object. Same-material voxels mesh no face between them, so the 7 floor cells and the rows below have no visible boundaries. Their sculpted corners also recompute when a neighbour breaks, so neighbouring outlines jump one frame after every break. In the shaft footprint from floor−3 to the floor: no sculpt offsets, and a 1-texel chisel seam at every cell boundary in the section path (35 % darker, with a 2 px lit lip toward the lantern), fading to 0 by 4 rows down. The ledge top face gets the same seams. Test: across a break, the pixel difference outside the broken cell's rect is ≤ 2 %.
6. Add V11 'valuables legibility' to §13.4. In every biome capture, ore, rich ore, gem and chest cells in the floor row and the next 2 rows reach ≥ 1.6:1 luminance or ≥ 25 ΔE2000 contrast against the host rock, and ≥ 1.3:1 at 4–5 rows down. feel.md had a 1.6:1 check and the SPEC dropped it. Without it, the 'promise' that justifies the cutaway can turn to mud.
7. Add rock albedo ramps to §11.2. The biome look table has none, although the section fills about 45 % of the screen. Each ramp's mid must sit ≥ 0.08 display luminance away from the biome fog colour so strata read against the haze.
8. State the textures for non-section faces and add a fallback (§11.3 terrainRig, §11.6 memory). The HD array holds only the section's 8 slots for 2 biomes. Fix the 2.6 MB budget line. Add a fallback for when a biome's HD layers are not painted yet (Plunge, Head Start, offline jump, setDepth): sample the 128² layer, never the wrong biome. Test: setDepth to each biome and capture its first frame.
