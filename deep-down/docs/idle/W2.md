## 3. The seams (publish these in the first 45 minutes)

1. **The section look, shared with B's shards (critic 2, MUST 9).** 2D publishes `src/render/look.ts`, exporting:
   - `SECTION_SHADE_GLSL`: a GLSL chunk with `vec3 sectionShade(vec2 cellUv, float blockId, vec3 worldPos, vec3 light)`, or whatever signature you choose, documented in the file;
   - `sectionUniforms()`, which returns the shared uniforms (the HD layer array, the ramps, the biome blend).

   B's `shardMaterial.ts` includes the chunk, so at F0 a shard looks exactly like the face it replaces. Publish a stub that compiles in the first 45 minutes. B builds against the stub.
2. **The post params C drives.** Keep `post.params.flash`, `flashColor` and `bloomStrength`. Add `dim` (0..1), `iris` (radius 0..1.5, with a centre), `shock` (centre, 0..1) and `bloomPulse` (0..1), each documented in `PostFX.ts`. 2D publishes these as fields first; C's ceremonies call them.
3. **Vistas and set pieces.**
   - B's `caverns.ts` decides where a Vista or Reveal Hall opens: rows, x-range, biome. It calls `scene.vistas.show({ id, biome, y0, y1, x0, x1 })` and `hide(id)` on a `VistaBackdrop` instance owned by `DescentScene`.
   - 2D's `VistaBackdrop` draws the backdrop layers and the light cones behind the opened back wall. 2D's `setPieces/<biome>.ts` export `buildSetPiece(biome, rng): { object: THREE.Object3D; footprint: { w: number; h: number } }`, and B places it inside the cavern.
   - 2D publishes the `VistaBackdrop` class shape and one set-piece stub in the first 45 minutes.
4. **Biome looks.** `biomeLooks.ts` holds every visual parameter: fog, key, rim, the LUT grade, accents and rock ramps. `BiomeLook.update(depth, blend)` keeps its signature. B reads looks, never writes them.

## 5. 2D's workstream: the HD look

**Goal at the gate.** Every capture reads as a premium mobile game: crisp at DPR 2–3, no visible texel stair-steps on the section, rich colour with depth, glow and contrast. Each of the 14 biomes reads as its own world at a glance. The surface at dusk is a key frame worthy of store screenshot 1. Read `docs/idle/designs/visual.md`, `wonder.md`, and SPEC §6 and §11 first.

**MUST:**
1. **The pipeline (critic 2, MUST 15, 12, 13, 16; SHOULD 5, 6, 7, 8; BUILD §4.2 MUST 1):**
   - a two-resolution render with a sub-viewport, a Catmull-Rom upsample, FXAA 3.11 before the upsample on Balanced, and CAS after AA;
   - the 32³ LUT pair in the sRGB-encoded domain, and the portrait vignette;
   - portrait presets: Battery, Balanced, High. Balanced has a composite DPR cap of 2.25 and a scene scale of 0.60–0.70.
   - Every variant is compiled at load (P3 holds across presets).
   - The clear colour is the blended fog. Post taps are clamped to the live rect.
   - The GPU memory budget is recomputed.
   - `tools/perf.mjs` gets the per-pass cost model and fails above SPEC §11.6.
2. **HD materials (critic 2, MUST 7, 8; SHOULD 3, 16):**
   - per-biome 5-stop rock ramps for all 14 biomes;
   - section painting at HD density (an HD array at 32–64 texels per metre, or procedural per-pixel strata and micro-detail in the section shader), so a 44 px cell shows smooth, crafted rock, not 16² tiles;
   - textures for non-section faces (cavern walls, the back wall, ledges), with a fallback;
   - a lantern specular on ore, gem and metal;
   - near-grain below 2 px per texel.

   V11 holds in every biome: valuables reach ≥ 1.6:1 luminance or ≥ 25 ΔE2000 against the host in rows 0–2. The colour of rich ore, gems and chests must make a player want to tap them.
3. **The 14 biome looks** in `biomeLooks.ts`: fog, key, rim, the LUT grade, emissive accents and ramps. V5 distinctness holds. The human amber is ≥ 25° of hue from the accents in warm biomes (SHOULD 11).
4. **Vistas and hero set pieces** for biomes 0–6, at least: 2 backdrop layers, light cones and one hero set piece per biome, from a hero brief for each (SHOULD 12). Biomes 7–13 next, if time allows.
5. **The surface at dusk as a key frame** (SHOULD 15): sky gradient, stars, the camp, depth and atmosphere.
6. **The beauty bar (critic 2, MUST 18):**
   - Write `docs/idle/LOOK.md`: a key-frame brief per biome, with palette swatches, a value plan for the four bands, the hero and the accents.
   - Run at least 3 capture rounds of `tools/look-capture.mjs` at 390 × 844 @2, plus 360 × 740 and 412 × 915 for three biomes.

## 6. The director's progression demands (2A and 2C)

Make the upgrade loop itself compulsive.
- Every purchase visibly changes the dig: a number, a tool form, a crew figure.
- Milestones every 10 and 25 levels give multipliers with a ceremony.
- NEXT★ shows how close the next one is ("3 levels to ×2"). BEST is always one thumb away.
- Achievements (Feats) pop with a satisfying toast, a sound and a reward.
- Rank-ups feel like events.

Every system must answer "what do I get next, and how soon?" on screen.
